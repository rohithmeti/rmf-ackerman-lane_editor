How to use

  1. Load the map (.yaml + .pgm).
  2. Place dock: press on the map and drag in the direction the robot faces, release.
  3. Draw lanes: move the mouse. Red circles = the robot's turning circles (unreachable). The green arc at the cursor distance = allowed angle range. Click to drop the next node; the curve and its micro-nodes are made for you.
  4. Shift snaps the turn to 15° steps (0° = straight, 90° = quarter turn).
  5. Click any dock/node to continue from it. Esc = stop drawing.
  6. Wheel = zoom, right/middle drag = pan.
